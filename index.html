About

陈愈潮，山东大学网络空间安全学院博士研究生，导师为王美琴教授，主要研究方向是工作模式的设计与分析、对称密码算法结构的自动化证明技术。

Chen Yuchao is a PhD student in the School of Cyber Science and Technology, Shandong University, supervised by Professor Wang Meiqin. His main research interests include the design and analysis of modes of operation, as well as automated proof techniques for symmetric cryptographic algorithm structures.

Research Interests

Authenticated Encryption, Modes of Operation, Security Proofs
